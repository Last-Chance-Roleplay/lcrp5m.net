CLAYTON COUNTY / WHITELISTED

Clayton County Sheriff's Office

The Sheriff's Office holds down everything outside the city. Deputies cover the county, from quiet back roads to small towns where everybody knows the patrol car.

If you want variety, this is the badge. One shift can go from a traffic stop to serving a warrant to booking a suspect at the county jail.

  • County patrol and traffic enforcement
  • Warrant service and civil process
  • Jail operations and transports
  • Courthouse security
  • Investigations and follow-ups
  • K9 and specialty units

STATEWIDE / WHITELISTED

Georgia State Patrol

Troopers own the highways. Georgia State Patrol works every freeway and state route, and backs up local agencies when things get serious.

Expect speed enforcement, crash investigations, DUI work and pursuits that start on the highway and end wherever the suspect runs out of road.

  • Highway and state route patrol
  • Speed and DUI enforcement
  • Crash investigation
  • Pursuit response
  • Commercial vehicle stops
  • Mutual aid to county and city units

SOUTH FULTON / WHITELISTED

South Fulton Police Department

South Fulton PD polices the city. Officers work the busiest streets on the server, close to the people they serve.

City policing means more calls, faster. Disturbances, store robberies, shots fired and traffic, all inside a beat where you get to know the regulars.

  • City beat patrol
  • 911 call response
  • Traffic enforcement on city streets
  • Community policing
  • Criminal investigations
  • Business and retail calls

CLAYTON COUNTY / WHITELISTED

Clayton County Fire & Emergency Services

Clayton County Fire & Emergency Services answers when something burns, crashes or stops breathing. Firefighters and medics run out of stations across the county.

It's a different kind of RP. Structure fires, highway wrecks, medical calls and transports to the hospital, all worked hand in hand with law enforcement on scene.

  • Structure and vehicle fires
  • Emergency medical response
  • Patient care and transport
  • Vehicle extrication
  • Hazmat and technical rescue
  • Scene support for law enforcement
