CCSO

Clayton County Sheriff's Office

COUNTY LAW ENFORCEMENT / WHITELISTED

The Sheriff's Office holds down Clayton County. Deputies work the unincorporated county, the county roads between cities, and everything that runs through the courthouse and the jail.

If you want variety, this is the badge. One shift can go from a traffic stop on Tara Blvd to serving a warrant in Riverdale to booking a suspect at the justice center.

  • County patrol and traffic enforcement
  • Warrant service and civil process
  • Jail operations and transports
  • Courthouse security
  • Investigations and follow-ups
  • K9 and specialty units

GSP

Georgia State Patrol

STATEWIDE TRAFFIC ENFORCEMENT / WHITELISTED

Troopers own the highways. Georgia State Patrol works I-75, I-85, I-285 and the state routes that tie the metro together, and backs up local agencies when things get serious.

Expect speed enforcement, crash investigations, DUI work and pursuits that start on the interstate and end wherever the suspect runs out of road.

  • Interstate and state route patrol
  • Speed and DUI enforcement
  • Crash investigation
  • Pursuit response
  • Commercial vehicle stops
  • Mutual aid to county and city units

SFPD

South Fulton Police Department

MUNICIPAL POLICE / WHITELISTED

South Fulton PD polices the city. Officers work Old National Hwy, Camp Creek, Cascade and the neighborhoods in between, close to the people they serve.

City policing means more calls, faster. Disturbances, shoplifting, shots fired and traffic, all inside a tight beat where you get to know the regulars.

  • City beat patrol
  • 911 call response
  • Traffic enforcement on city streets
  • Community policing
  • Criminal investigations
  • Business and retail calls

FIRE

Clayton County Fire & Emergency Services

FIRE, RESCUE AND EMS / WHITELISTED

Clayton County Fire & Emergency Services answers when something burns, crashes or stops breathing. Firefighters and medics run out of stations across the county.

It's a different kind of RP. Structure fires, extrications on I-75, medical calls and transports to the hospital, all worked hand in hand with law enforcement on scene.

  • Structure and vehicle fires
  • Emergency medical response
  • Patient care and transport
  • Vehicle extrication
  • Hazmat and technical rescue
  • Scene support for law enforcement
