About the city

Welcome to Last Chance

We are a QBox and ERS (Emergency Response Simulator) server built around economy-based jobs for our departments and a civilian side that's open to everyone.

We host events to bring the community together, and we want to hear what you'd like to see. Drop your ideas in #server-revamp-suggestions on the Discord.

As Last Chance grows, the community shapes where it goes. If something can be better, tell us.

Getting in

New to the city?

Here's the short version. Read the rules before you load in. Not knowing them isn't an excuse.

  1. Join the Discord

    Hop into discord.gg/lcrp5m and verify your account.

  2. Read the rules

    Everything is on the rules page. Learn it before you play.

  3. Build a character

    Give them a name, a backstory and a reason to be here.

  4. Play it out

    Stay in character, give others good RP and let the story happen. Want a badge? Apply in the Discord.

FAQ

Quick answers

Still stuck? Open a ticket in the Discord. Please don't DM staff directly for support.

What is our connect code?

Open FiveM, press F8 and run connect play.lcrp5m.net. You can also search for Last Chance Roleplay in the server browser.

What CAD and radio do we use?

We run our own custom-built CAD at cad.lcrp5m.net, tied into ERS, and Sonoran Radio for comms.

Is voice chat required?

Yes. Voice chat is required to keep interactions realistic and immersive. Push-to-talk is required in the city.

How do I join a department?

Every department is whitelisted. Submit an application in the Discord for the agency you want. Civilian is open to everyone, no application needed.

How old do I have to be?

You must be at least 16 to play on Last Chance.
